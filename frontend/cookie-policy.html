BITASEC Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how BITASEC uses cookies and similar technologies on its website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites may store in a visitor's browser. Similar technologies can also store information or help websites understand how their services are used.

2. Essential technologies

Some storage may be necessary for basic website functions, security, and remembering your privacy preferences.

3. Analytics cookies

If BITASEC enables analytics services, these may help us understand website traffic and improve our services. Such optional technologies should only be activated in accordance with your consent and applicable law.

4. Marketing cookies

Marketing technologies may be used to measure advertising effectiveness or provide relevant advertisements. They should remain disabled unless you have given the required permission.

5. Managing your preferences

You can use the Cookie Preferences button on the website to change your choices. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

6. Current implementation

The website's consent interface provides controls for essential, analytics, and marketing categories. The availability of actual cookies depends on the services integrated into the website. This policy must be reviewed and updated to accurately describe any analytics, advertising, or third-party technologies in use.

7. Contact BITASEC

For privacy questions, contact bitasectechnology@gmail.com.
